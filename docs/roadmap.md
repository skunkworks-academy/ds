# 12-Month Roadmap

The roadmap moves from validating Desh's existing senior baseline into architecture/security depth, repeatable engineering, governance and career conversion. The first 12 weeks are deliberately detailed; later months are evidence-driven and may be adjusted at formal review gates.

## Phase map

| Phase | Months | Focus | Required outcome | Evidence target |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Foundation validation | 1–2 | Baseline, role positioning, architecture principles, GitHub workflow | Approved capability baseline and first architecture artefact | Capability matrix, ADR set, GitHub evidence workflow |
| Security architecture | 3–4 | Zero Trust, identity, endpoint, SIEM/XDR and incident response | Security reference architecture and SOC detection pack | Zero Trust architecture, endpoint baseline, detection/IR pack |
| Automation & cloud | 5–6 | PowerShell, GitHub Actions, Bicep/Terraform, Azure/AWS architecture | Tested automation toolkit and IaC baseline | Automation project, CI workflow, IaC baseline, cloud ADRs |
| Governance & resilience | 7–8 | Risk, controls, cost, backup/DR, vendor and change governance | Governance pack and resilience design | Risk register, control matrix, recovery design, RACI |
| Leadership & strategy | 9–10 | Business case, budgeting, stakeholder alignment, mentoring | Strategic modernisation proposal and team-enablement evidence | Executive brief, staged roadmap, knowledge-sharing evidence |
| Career conversion | 11–12 | Portfolio, credential review, interview stories, leadership presentation | Final portfolio, capability scorecard and next-role plan | Portfolio index, interview stories, 10-minute presentation, annual score |

## Months 1–2 — Foundation validation

**Objectives**
- confirm primary/secondary role direction;
- validate sustainable weekly learning load;
- turn existing experience into a capability baseline;
- establish ADR, GitHub and mentor-review patterns;
- refresh current credential/references register.

**Completion gate**
At least one accepted architecture artefact, three or more weekly evidence items, working review workflow and no unresolved privacy/security concerns.

## Months 3–4 — Security architecture

**Objectives**
- integrate Entra/Zero Trust identity, endpoint controls and security operations;
- design SOC detection and incident-response logic;
- demonstrate security architecture as an end-to-end system rather than separate product settings.

**Completion gate**
Zero Trust architecture and SOC/detection pack each score at least 3/5; one incident tabletop is complete; at least two portfolio artefacts are under review.

## Months 5–6 — Automation and cloud

**Objectives**
- make safe automation a repeatable engineering practice;
- build one CI workflow with explicit permissions;
- create a non-production Bicep/Terraform baseline;
- strengthen Azure operational architecture and AWS comparison literacy.

**Completion gate**
One tested automation, one IaC baseline and one cloud reference architecture are accepted with rollback/teardown guidance.

## Months 7–8 — Governance and resilience

**Objectives**
- translate technical exposure into risk, control ownership and evidence;
- connect architecture decisions to cost, recovery, vendor and change governance;
- test resilience assumptions through safe tabletop exercises.

**Completion gate**
Risk/control pack and recovery/resilience design are mentor-reviewed; exceptions/owners/review dates are explicit.

## Months 9–10 — Leadership and strategy

**Objectives**
- write decision-ready security/IT modernisation proposals;
- define 90/180/365-day delivery roadmap and budget assumptions;
- practice stakeholder/RACI mapping;
- complete at least one knowledge-sharing/mentoring session.

**Completion gate**
One executive modernisation brief and one presentation score at least 4/5 for communication; one mentoring/knowledge-sharing item is evidenced.

## Months 11–12 — Career conversion

**Objectives**
- consolidate six portfolio artefacts;
- confirm credential/skill milestones that remain current and useful;
- create four evidence-linked interview stories;
- rehearse senior architecture/security narrative;
- complete final scorecard and next-role plan.

**Completion gate**
Portfolio is review-ready, evidence links are traceable, annual score is at least 4/5 in every dimension, and the mentor agrees the next-role plan.

## Formal review gates

### Day 30

- learning cadence is sustainable;
- GitHub evidence workflow is working;
- first architecture artefact reviewed;
- current credential map confirmed;
- next 30-day priorities agreed.

### Day 60

- Zero Trust and SOC evidence demonstrates architecture-level reasoning;
- at least one automation has tests and rollback guidance;
- at least two portfolio artefacts are in mentor review;
- no persistent privacy/scope issue exists.

### Day 90

- at least 10 of 12 sprint weeks are accepted or a documented recovery plan exists;
- capstone scope is approved;
- at least three portfolio artefacts are review-ready;
- primary role target is selected for the next nine months.

## Annual completion gate

The IDR is complete when Desh can **explain, demonstrate and defend** the portfolio outputs in a senior technical or management interview—not merely when courses are marked complete.
