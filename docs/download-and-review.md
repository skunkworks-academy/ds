## Formal review gates

### Day 30

The first gate checks whether the plan is executable rather than aspirational.

- learning cadence is sustainable;
- at least three weekly evidence items exist;
- GitHub evidence workflow is working;
- first architecture artefact has mentor feedback;
- credential priorities remain current;
- no privacy/security boundary has been breached.

**Decision:** continue, reduce scope, increase support, or redesign the next 30 days.

### Day 60

- Zero Trust and endpoint evidence show architecture-level reasoning;
- SOC/detection work maps threats, data and triage decisions coherently;
- at least one automation has tests, logging and rollback guidance;
- at least two portfolio artefacts have mentor review;
- workload remains realistic.

**Decision:** confirm capstone direction and the highest-value credential/skill milestone.

### Day 90

- at least 10 of 12 sprint weeks are accepted or a documented recovery plan exists;
- at least three portfolio artefacts are review-ready;
- primary role target is explicit;
- next nine months are prioritised by evidence gaps, not course volume;
- capstone scope is approved.

**Decision:** move into the longer 12-month build phase or revise role direction.

## Annual completion gate

The IDR is complete when Desh can **explain, demonstrate and defend** the work in a senior technical or management interview. Course attendance alone is not completion.

Target end-state:

1. two reviewed security/cloud architectures;
2. six portfolio artefacts;
3. two tested automation/IaC deliverables;
4. one complete SOC/detection/incident-response pack;
5. one risk/governance pack;
6. two executive-ready communications;
7. four core plus four supporting/applied credential milestones;
8. a final mentor score of at least 4/5 in each IDR review dimension.

## Review questions

- Is the senior-role positioning still accurate?
- Is 3–4 hours weekly sustainable?
- Are the selected labs authorised, safe and relevant?
- Does the portfolio demonstrate architecture and leadership rather than only administration?
- Are credential targets current and worth the effort?
- Are the KPIs measurable enough to support a 90-day decision?
- Are assumptions, trade-offs and residual risks explicit?
- Can a reviewer trace each claimed capability to evidence?
