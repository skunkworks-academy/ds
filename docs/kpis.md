# Goals and KPIs

The IDR measures accepted evidence and decision quality. Attendance, hours and course completion are supporting indicators only.

## Outcome KPIs

| KPI | Target | Measurement | Review cadence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Sprint execution | ≥10 of 12 weeks accepted | Completed evidence meeting weekly acceptance criteria | Weekly + Day 90 |
| Portfolio evidence | 6 reviewed artefacts | Mentor-reviewed repositories/documents | Fortnightly |
| Security architecture | 2 reviewed architectures | Zero Trust + hybrid/cloud/security designs scored against rubric | Day 60 / 90 / annual |
| Automation | 2 tested automations | Tests, logs, safe defaults, permissions and rollback | Monthly |
| SOC capability | 1 complete detection/IR pack | Detections, triage, metrics, ATT&CK mapping and incident response | Day 60 / 90 |
| Governance | 1 risk/control pack | Risks, controls, ownership, evidence, exceptions and review dates | Day 90 |
| Leadership communication | 2 executive briefs/presentations | Clear business value, trade-offs and decision request | Quarterly |
| Credential progress | 4 core + 4 supporting/applied milestones | Current vendor learning records or accepted evidence score ≥4 | Quarterly |
| Mentoring contribution | 2 knowledge-sharing sessions | Slides, notes, demo or peer feedback | By annual review |
| Career conversion | 4 STAR/architecture interview stories | Evidence-linked narratives reviewed by mentor | Months 9–12 |

## Development goals

### Goal 1 — Architecture maturity

**Target:** move from strong operational implementation experience to explicit, reviewable architecture ownership.

**Measures**
- minimum two architecture packs;
- minimum six ADRs across the year;
- assumptions, trade-offs, resilience, security and operational ownership documented;
- mentor score ≥4/5 by annual review.

### Goal 2 — Security operations leadership

**Target:** demonstrate the ability to design detection/incident operating logic, not only use security tooling.

**Measures**
- three or more tested synthetic detections;
- ATT&CK mapping and triage process;
- incident tabletop and lessons learned;
- SOC metrics with definitions and decision purpose;
- mentor score ≥4/5 in security reasoning.

### Goal 3 — Automation and reproducibility

**Target:** make repeatable engineering a visible part of Desh's senior capability.

**Measures**
- one PowerShell automation project with tests/logging/rollback;
- one Bicep/Terraform baseline;
- one CI validation workflow;
- no hard-coded secrets;
- documented permissions and teardown/recovery.

### Goal 4 — Governance and business translation

**Target:** connect technical change to risk, ownership, cost and decision-making.

**Measures**
- risk register and control/evidence matrix;
- executive modernisation brief;
- staged roadmap and RACI;
- at least two decision-ready presentations/briefs.

### Goal 5 — Career conversion

**Target:** convert the work into evidence that supports senior architecture/security/IT-management opportunities.

**Measures**
- six portfolio artefacts indexed in one portfolio README;
- four interview-ready stories;
- one 10-minute architecture presentation;
- final capability review and next-role plan.

## Formal review scorecard

Score each dimension from 1–5.

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Architecture quality | Mostly descriptive; weak decisions | Coherent design with justified choices | Senior-level trade-offs, failure modes and reusable decisions |
| Security reasoning | Tool/config focused | Risks/threats and controls are connected | Threat/risk reasoning drives architecture and residual-risk decisions |
| Automation quality | Ad-hoc / untested | Repeatable with tests and documentation | Safe-by-default, governed, observable and reusable |
| Governance | Ownership/evidence unclear | Risks, controls and owners documented | Decision-ready governance with exceptions, review and evidence lifecycle |
| Communication | Technically correct but hard to consume | Audience-appropriate and structured | Concise executive/technical communication that drives a decision |
| Leadership | Passive completion | Owns work and responds to feedback | Enables others, mentors, challenges assumptions and improves the operating model |

**Annual target:** 4/5 or higher in every dimension.

## Review thresholds

### Day 30

- ≥3 weekly evidence items accepted;
- learning cadence sustainable;
- first architecture artefact reviewed;
- no privacy/security violations;
- priority credential map current.

### Day 60

- ≥6 weekly evidence items accepted;
- Zero Trust/endpoint evidence scored ≥3/5;
- SOC evidence scored ≥3/5;
- one tested automation accepted;
- at least two portfolio artefacts under mentor review.

### Day 90

- ≥10 of 12 sprint weeks accepted **or** a documented recovery plan;
- at least three portfolio artefacts review-ready;
- primary role target confirmed;
- capstone direction approved;
- next nine months prioritised by evidence gaps.

## Red/amber/green status

| Status | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Evidence on track; workload sustainable | Continue plan |
| Amber | One or two missed evidence outcomes, quality below target, or workload strain | Adjust scope/cadence with mentor within two weeks |
| Red | Repeated missed evidence, privacy/security breach, no review response, or plan no longer supports target role | Pause/re-plan before accumulating more work |

## Evidence rule

A KPI must be traceable to a file, repository, review note, learning record or other verifiable artefact. Self-reported completion without evidence does not move the KPI.
