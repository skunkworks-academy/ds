## Weekly timetable

| Day | Time | Activity | Purpose | Expected evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | 18:30–19:15 | Focused theory / vendor learning | Learn one senior-level concept and identify a design decision | Notes + source link + one question/decision |
| Wednesday | 18:30–19:15 | Guided lab planning | Define scope, assumptions, architecture, data boundary and test plan | Lab plan / issue |
| Thursday | 18:30–19:30 | Build / lab execution | Produce working technical evidence | Code, configuration, test output or diagram |
| Saturday | 09:00–10:00 | Deep work, documentation and portfolio | Finish the weekly artefact and reflection | README, evidence, lessons learned |
| Alternate Wednesday | 19:30–20:00 | Mentor review | Review evidence, blockers, KPI movement and next decision | Review note + next actions |

The base cadence is approximately **3 hours 30 minutes per week**, with an additional 30-minute mentor session every second week. The average across a two-week cycle is approximately **3 hours 45 minutes per week**, within the 3–4 hour target. The mentor may adjust the timetable when operational duties make a particular week unrealistic, but any change should preserve the weekly evidence outcome.

## Google Calendar

1. Download the `.ics` file for a one-time import, **or** open Google Calendar.
2. Go to **Other calendars → + → From URL**.
3. Paste the HTTPS subscription URL shown above.
4. Confirm that Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Saturday and fortnightly review sessions appear.

## Outlook

1. Open Outlook Calendar.
2. Choose **Add calendar → Subscribe from web**.
3. Paste the HTTPS subscription URL.
4. Give the calendar a name such as `Desh IDR — 12 Week Sprint`.
5. Save and verify the first week beginning 31 August 2026.

## Review checkpoints

- **Weekly:** update progress, evidence link and blockers.
- **Fortnightly:** mentor reviews one completed artefact and the next two-week priority.
- **Day 30:** confirm the learning cadence and first architecture evidence.
- **Day 60:** confirm Zero Trust/SOC quality and at least one tested automation.
- **Day 90:** confirm primary role direction, capstone scope and at least three reviewed portfolio artefacts.

## Rescheduling rule

A missed session is not automatically a failed week. Move the session to the nearest practical slot, preserve the required evidence, and record the reason in the progress workspace. Repeated schedule slippage should trigger a mentor discussion and workload adjustment rather than hidden backlog.
