## 1. Development placement

**Primary pathway:** Cloud & Cybersecurity Solution Architect / Senior Technical Lead  
**Secondary pathway:** Infrastructure Manager / Cybersecurity Manager / IT Manager  
**Mentor / facilitator:** Raydo Matthee  
**Initial workload:** approximately 3–4 focused hours per week  
**Initial execution cycle:** 12 weeks  
**Long-term review horizon:** August 2026 to August 2027

The roadmap assumes substantial prior enterprise infrastructure, endpoint, systems-administration and Microsoft-platform experience. It therefore focuses on architecture, security leadership, automation, governance, business decision-making and demonstrable evidence rather than repeating entry-level support material.

## 2. Established capability baseline

The public capability profile recognises experience across:

- enterprise infrastructure and endpoint management;
- Microsoft 365, Azure, Intune, SCCM/MDT, Active Directory and Exchange;
- Windows endpoint modernisation and deployment;
- Microsoft Defender and Sentinel exposure;
- networking, firewalls, VPNs, DNS, DHCP, routing and switching;
- virtualisation, backup, disaster recovery and infrastructure maintenance;
- incident, problem, change and service-management practices;
- senior troubleshooting and escalation handling;
- migrations, proof-of-concepts, project coordination and technical planning;
- stakeholder communication, vendor collaboration and mentoring.

The development goal is not to prove that Desh can administer these technologies. It is to demonstrate that he can **design, govern, automate, secure, defend and explain** them at senior technical/management level.

## 3. 12-month North Star

> By August 2027, Desh should be able to design and defend a secure hybrid-cloud architecture, lead a practical SOC or security-modernisation initiative, automate repeatable infrastructure controls, communicate technology risk and investment decisions to leadership, and demonstrate a credible portfolio for senior cloud, infrastructure, cybersecurity or IT-management roles.

## 4. Development priorities

1. Advanced cloud and hybrid architecture across Microsoft-first environments with cross-cloud literacy.
2. SOC engineering, SIEM/XDR, threat detection and incident-response leadership.
3. Zero Trust, identity, endpoint and cloud-governance architecture.
4. PowerShell, Infrastructure as Code and secure delivery automation.
5. Risk management, governance, resilience and business-case communication.
6. Cross-functional project leadership, mentoring and executive presentation.
7. Portfolio evidence that shows senior-level decision quality rather than tool familiarity alone.

- AZ-104 readiness;
- cross-cloud architecture literacy;

- PowerShell automation evidence;

The previously listed AZ-500 credential retires on **31 August 2026**. Its objectives may still be used as a short-term security-learning reference, but a replacement/successor credential should be selected from Microsoft's current catalogue after retirement.

## 12. KPI model

The roadmap measures:

- sprint execution;
- portfolio evidence;
- architecture quality;
- automation quality;
- SOC capability;
- governance/risk work;
- leadership communication;
- credential progress;
- mentoring contribution;
- career-conversion evidence.

Formal reviews use a 1–5 score across architecture quality, security reasoning, automation quality, governance, communication and leadership. The annual target is **4/5 or higher in every dimension**. See [Goals & KPIs](/idr/kpis/).

## 14. Review gates

### Day 30

Confirm cadence, evidence workflow, first architecture output and current credential priorities.

### Day 60

Confirm Zero Trust/SOC quality, at least one tested automation and at least two reviewed portfolio artefacts.

### Day 90

Confirm at least 10 accepted sprint weeks or a recovery plan, at least three review-ready portfolio artefacts, a clear primary role target and approved capstone scope.

### Annual review

The roadmap is complete when Desh can explain, demonstrate and defend the evidence in a senior technical or management interview.

## 15. Governance and privacy

Public evidence must not contain:

- passwords, tokens, private keys or secrets;
- customer or employer confidential information;
- production logs containing sensitive data;
- private architecture not authorised for publication;
- personal contact or financial information;
- unauthorised screenshots or exports.

Use synthetic data and explicitly authorised lab environments. See [Governance](/idr/governance/) for the full evidence boundary.
