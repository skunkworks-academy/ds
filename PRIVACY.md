# Privacy and source-data boundary

The IDR was derived from Deshan Singh's career assessment and professional profile. Source documents may contain private contact and personal information. The public site intentionally excludes private contact details, banking data and detailed source-form responses.

## Never commit

- account numbers, statements or transaction histories;
- personal phone numbers or private email addresses;
- passwords, tokens, keys or credentials;
- customer, employee or employer-confidential data;
- production security logs containing identifying information;
- unredacted screenshots from enterprise systems.

Portfolio evidence must be synthetic, sanitised or expressly authorised for publication.
